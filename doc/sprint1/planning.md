# 2. Technical decisions and coordination #

It was discussed in group that the copper cable wiring standard to be adopted is *CAT7* with the color pattern *T-568B* for being the most widely adopted in the industry, facilitating the acquisition of the components
and future maintenance of the cabling.

It was decided that the Campus Backbone (all MC–IC connections) and the Vertical Backbone of the terminals would be entirely structured using optical fiber.

Redundant links were implemented in the important connections between ICs and HCs to maintain a professional structure in the project.
